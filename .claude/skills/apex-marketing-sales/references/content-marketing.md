# Content Marketing

## Scope
Using educational and entertaining content to attract and retain customers: blogging, guides, case studies, webinars, and owned media.

## Core principles
- Content marketing works on three levels: awareness (blog articles on "how to do X" bring search traffic), consideration (guides and comparisons help buyers evaluate options), and decision (case studies and ROI calculators drive action).
- Distribution matters more than production; a mediocre article shared to the right audience (email list, newsletter, Slack communities) outperforms a brilliant article with no distribution.
- Owned media (blog, newsletter, YouTube channel) builds moats; paid media (ads) can disappear overnight if budgets cut, while owned media compounds in SEO value and list growth.
- Content ROI is lagged; expect 2-3 month lags between content publish and lead impact; companies that stop content too early never see the payoff.
- Topic authority (becoming known for expertise on a narrow topic) drives business outcomes more than content volume; 10 deep articles on "enterprise security audits" beat 100 surface-level tips.

## Apex practices
- Build a content calendar aligned to the buyer journey (awareness content for early-stage, decision content for late-stage); production without strategy wastes effort.
- Create content series (5-part guide to X) instead of one-offs; series drive more engagement and allow deeper exploration.
- Repurpose content across formats (blog → email course → webinar → podcast episode); different buyers consume different formats.
- Measure content ROI via attribution (which leads came from which content?) not just page views; page views are vanity if they don't lead to customers.

## Pitfalls
- Publishing content infrequently (one post per quarter); algorithms favor consistency and momentum.
- Writing for the search engine instead of the human; keyword-stuffed garbage ranks temporarily but doesn't convert.
- Creating content without a distribution plan; "if you build it, they will come" is false for most content.
