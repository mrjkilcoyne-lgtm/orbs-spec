# SEO Strategy

## Scope
Optimizing for search engine visibility: technical SEO, keyword research, backlink strategy, and measuring organic impact.

## Core principles
- Google ranks pages (not sites) on relevance, authority, and user experience; relevance comes from keyword targeting, authority from backlinks and domain age, UX from Core Web Vitals (page speed, layout stability, interactivity).
- Keyword demand determines ceiling; optimizing for "the best project management tool" (high volume, high competition) takes 10x longer to rank than "project management for biotech labs" (lower volume, less competition).
- E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals matter; a blog written by your CEO on their personal story beats a generic How-To, especially in high-stakes categories (health, finance).
- Backlinks are citations; pages with more quality citations rank higher. One link from Harvard is worth 100 links from spam sites.
- Algorithm changes (Google's core updates) can kill organic traffic overnight; relying 100% on organic leads is risky without diversification (paid, sales, partnerships).

## Apex practices
- Start with keyword research (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz) targeting long-tail keywords (less competition, higher intent) where you can realistically rank in 6 months.
- Audit existing content to identify quick wins (pages ranking 6-20th for keywords — add depth, improve UX, target better keywords, and you can break top 3).
- Build backlinks through valuable content (people naturally link to your work), PR outreach, and guest posts; never buy links (Google penalizes).
- Measure organic ROI via lead attribution and revenue impact, not just rankings and traffic; a high-ranking page that doesn't convert is expensive.

## Pitfalls
- Optimizing for high-volume keywords that don't match buyer intent; ranking for "free project management tools" doesn't help if you sell enterprise software.
- Publishing thin, low-effort content and expecting to rank; Google prioritizes depth and uniqueness.
- Ignoring technical SEO (site speed, crawlability, structured data); a well-written article won't rank if Google can't crawl it.

## Tools & references
Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz Pro (keyword research and backlink analysis), Google Search Console (owned data), Screaming Frog (technical SEO audit), Brian Dean "The Skyscraper Technique."
