# Brand Strategy

## Scope
Building a distinctive brand identity: values, voice, visual identity, and brand consistency across touchpoints.

## Core principles
- Brand is the sum of experiences and emotions associated with your company; it's built through thousands of small interactions (good customer support, consistent visual identity, clear messaging) not through a logo.
- Brand premium (price uplift enabled by brand strength) is measurable; a company with strong brand can charge 10-20% more than a commodity alternative for the same product.
- Brand differentiation must be durable and authentic; a brand claiming "we care about customers" is noise unless backed by practices (hiring great support, product roadmap decisions). Inauthenticity kills trust faster than no claim at all.
- Visual identity (logo, color palette, typography) works on non-conscious recognition; seeing Stripe orange or Apple's minimalism creates instant associations.
- Brand loyalty is built through consistency and time; expect 2-3 years of consistent brand expression before recognition and reputation shift.

## Apex practices
- Define brand pillars (3-5 core values that define how you operate: Stripe's "simplicity," Patagonia's "environmentalism") and use them to guide decisions (hiring, partnerships, marketing spend).
- Create a brand book (guidelines for voice, tone, visual identity, usage of logo, color palette, typography) and distribute to all teams; consistency compounds.
- Invest in founder narrative and authenticity; founders like Paul Graham (Y Combinator), Elon Musk (Tesla, SpaceX), and Tony Hsieh (Zappos) built company brands through personal brand.
- Build brand through actions not claims; ship quickly, over-deliver on product, treat customers exceptionally well — these build reputation more than advertising.

## Pitfalls
- Changing visual identity frequently (new logo every 2 years) when consistency would compound; Apple's logo evolution is subtle and rare.
- Claiming brand values (sustainability, diversity) without backing them up; "virtue signaling" without action destroys trust when discovered.
- Spending heavily on brand advertising without product quality; no amount of ads fix a bad product experience.

## Tools & references
Marty Neumeier "Brand Gap," David Aaker "Building Strong Brands," brand audit frameworks (Interbrand), Brandwatch (brand monitoring), Figma (design systems).
