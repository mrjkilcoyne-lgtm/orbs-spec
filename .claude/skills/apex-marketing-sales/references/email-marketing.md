# Email Marketing

## Scope
Using email to nurture leads and retain customers: segmentation, automation, copywriting, and compliance.

## Core principles
- Email is the highest-ROI channel available (30-40x ROI for many businesses); it's owned media (you own the list), not rented like social or search.
- Segmentation (sending different emails to different audiences) drives 5-10x higher engagement than batch-and-blast; segment by behavior (signup source, purchase history, engagement level).
- Frequency matters; too often and you're spam, too infrequent and subscribers forget they signed up. Test 1x/week, 2x/week, 1x/month and measure unsubscribe rate and engagement decline.
- List quality deteriorates over time (emails bounce, users churn); email platforms report deliverability rates. A decaying deliverability rate means you need list hygiene.
- Automation (triggered emails based on user behavior) scales personal communication; "welcome" emails sent 5 minutes after signup drive 50%+ higher activation than batch sends.

## Apex practices
- Build a welcome series (5-7 emails over 14 days) triggered on signup; this is your highest-ROI investment and should be tested and refined continuously.
- Segment your list by funnel stage (prospect, customer, churned) and send different content; prospects get educational content, customers get product tips, churned users get win-back campaigns.
- A/B test subject lines and send times; open rate differences of 10-30% are common with different subject lines, and optimal send time varies by audience.
- Measure email ROI via link clicks (engagement), subscription → purchase (conversion), and email-attributed revenue; email is measurable end-to-end.

## Pitfalls
- Buying email lists; high unsubscribe and complaint rates tank deliverability. Grow your own list organically.
- Sending one-off campaigns to your list without strategy; email value compounds with consistency and relevance.
- Ignoring compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL); legal emails include clear unsubscribe, physical address, and opt-in records.
