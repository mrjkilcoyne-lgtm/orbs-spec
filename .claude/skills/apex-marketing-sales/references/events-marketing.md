# Events Marketing

## Scope
In-person and virtual events to drive awareness, lead generation, and customer engagement: conferences, webinars, user conferences, and roundtables.

## Core principles
- Events are ROI-negative on their own; the value is in the relationships and deal velocity acceleration they create (conferences compress 12 months of networking into 3 days).
- Event ROI varies by format: trade shows have low conversion (fishing expedition), user conferences have high LTV (customer expansion), webinars have moderate conversion (low-cost at scale).
- Event lead quality beats volume; 100 qualified leads from a targeted roundtable beat 1,000 low-quality leads from a booth at a massive conference.
- Post-event follow-up is where 80% of value is captured; a follow-up call within 24 hours from the person who met the prospect closes 3x faster than a generic email a week later.
- Virtual events are cheaper at scale but lack serendipity (hallway conversations); hybrid (in-person core + virtual access) is increasingly the standard.

## Apex practices
- Calculate event ROI per channel (budget spent, leads generated, deal pipeline created, customers closed) and focus on high-ROI events year after year; don't chase shiny new events.
- Train your booth/session staff to collect emails and book discovery calls (not just business cards); this enables immediate follow-up.
- Create a 90-day post-event playbook (when to follow up, what to send, who from your team follows up) so leads aren't lost to follow-up vacuum.
- For hosted events (conferences, roundtables you run), measure attendee quality (engagement, post-event conversion) not just head count.

## Pitfalls
- Attending events without a clear goal (lead generation, customer expansion, awareness?) and measuring only traffic or cost per lead.
- Letting booth duty fall to non-closers; your best salespeople should be at events talking to prospects, not demos to time-wasters.
- Failing to follow up; the best conference booth doesn't matter if leads aren't called within 48 hours.
