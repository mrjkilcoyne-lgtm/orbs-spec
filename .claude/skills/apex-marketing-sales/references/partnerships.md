# Partnerships

## Scope
Strategic and commercial partnerships to drive customer acquisition and expansion: channel partners, technology integrations, and go-to-market collaborations.

## Core principles
- Partnerships are about mutual benefit; if only one party wins, it won't scale. Define what each partner gains (customers, revenue, product capability) upfront.
- Partner channel CAC is often lower than direct sales; a partner selling your product as an add-on to their customer base is cheaper to acquire than building a sales team.
- Partner enablement (training, sales materials, commission structure) determines success; partners have no incentive to sell if they're not equipped or compensated.
- Tech partnerships (integrations, data connectors, embeds) expand product value without engineering cost; Stripe's API partnerships (Shopify, Twilio, etc.) drove adoption exponentially.
- Partners can have competing interests; a partner selling your product and your competitor's product will naturally push the competitor if margins are similar.

## Apex practices
- Start with 1-2 pilot partners (small scale, specific geography or use case) before scaling; learn what works before betting the company on channel.
- Create a partner playbook (how to sell the product, positioning for their customers, support model, commission/pricing) and update based on partner feedback.
- Implement partner portals (training materials, go-to-market resources, deal registration) so partners can self-serve and feel supported.
- Measure partnership ROI (customer acquisition via partner, partner revenue, customer quality); some partners acquire customers faster but with lower retention.

## Pitfalls
- Signing too many partners and spreading support thin; quality over quantity — 10 great partners beat 100 mediocre ones.
- Misaligned incentives (you want long-term customer relationships, partner wants one-time commission); this creates channel conflict.
- Assuming integration is a partnership; integrations are table stakes. Partnerships require go-to-market collaboration, not just API connectivity.

## Tools & references
Partnering with top channel partners (check their case studies), partner management platforms (Impartner, Jigsaw), partner compensation frameworks, technology partnership programs (AWS Partner Network, Salesforce AppExchange).
