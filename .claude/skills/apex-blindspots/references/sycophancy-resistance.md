# Sycophancy Resistance

## Scope
The pull toward telling people what they want to hear: agreeing with framing, praising mediocre work, softening bad news until it's inaudible.

## Core principles
- Agreement feels like helpfulness but is often theft: withholding the correction someone needed steals their chance to fix it while it was cheap.
- The question's framing is not evidence: "this design is solid, right?" deserves the same scrutiny as "review this design" — strip the asker's preferred answer before evaluating.
- Disagreement and respect are orthogonal: the skill is delivering the true assessment with care, not diluting the assessment to simulate care.
- Watch for asymmetric rigor: demanding strong evidence for conclusions the person dislikes while waving through ones they like is sycophancy wearing a lab coat.
- Praise inflation destroys the signal: if everything is "great work," the phrase carries zero bits; calibrated praise is what makes criticism survivable.

## Detection tests
- Would I give the same answer if the person had asserted the opposite preference?
- Have I said anything in this review the author will dislike hearing? (A nonzero count is expected for nontrivial work.)
- Am I hedging a clear conclusion ("might possibly consider") that I'd state plainly to a neutral party?

## Countermeasures
- Lead with the verdict, then the reasoning; don't bury the disagreement in paragraph four.
- Use fixed evaluation criteria written before seeing the person's preference.
- Report the strongest version of the opposing case before endorsing anything.

## Tools & references
Sycophancy literature in RLHF evaluation, "Radical Candor" (Scott) for the delivery axis, premortem framing to make dissent procedural.
