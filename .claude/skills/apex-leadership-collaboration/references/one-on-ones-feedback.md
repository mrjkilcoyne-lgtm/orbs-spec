# One-on-Ones & Feedback

## Scope
Running effective recurring 1:1s and delivering feedback that changes behavior without destroying trust.

## Core principles
- The 1:1 is the report's meeting, not yours — status belongs in standups and dashboards; the 1:1 is for the things that have no other channel: friction, ambition, doubt.
- Feedback has a half-life measured in days: praise or correct within the week or the specific behavior is unrecoverable and you're critiquing a person, not an action.
- SBI structure (Situation-Behavior-Impact) keeps feedback falsifiable: "In Tuesday's design review (S), you dismissed the intern's question mid-sentence (B), and she didn't speak again (I)" — no mind-reading, no character verdicts.
- Radical Candor's two axes — care personally and challenge directly — fail independently: challenge without care is obnoxious aggression; care without challenge is ruinous empathy, and ruinous empathy is the more common failure in engineering orgs.
- Psychological safety (Edmondson) is the precondition: people only surface bad news, half-formed ideas, and mistakes if doing so has never been punished — and one public punishment resets the counter to zero.

## Apex practices
- Keep a shared running doc per report; both parties add agenda items during the week — an empty agenda after 10 minutes means ask "how's life outside work?" and mean it.
- Never cancel 1:1s when busy; reschedule same-week. Cancellation frequency is read (correctly) as a priority signal.
- Ask "what feedback do you have for me?" every time, tolerate the silence, and visibly act on the first thing offered — that act buys all future honesty.
- Separate feedback conversations from ratings conversations; the moment compensation is in the room, listening stops.

## Pitfalls
- The feedback sandwich — recipients learn to ignore the bread and hear only doom in every compliment.
- Saving up feedback for the performance review; nothing in a review should ever be a surprise, and surprise is a manager failure, not a report failure.
- Letting 1:1s decay into status meetings, then wondering why the resignation blindsided you.
