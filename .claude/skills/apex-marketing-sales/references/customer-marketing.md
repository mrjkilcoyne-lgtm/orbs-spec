# Customer Marketing

## Scope
Marketing to existing customers: retention, expansion, referral programs, and customer advocacy.

## Core principles
- Customer marketing (retention + expansion) has 5-10x higher ROI than new customer acquisition; investing in existing customers pays back immediately while new customer investment has 12+ month payback.
- Churn is exponential: a company losing 3% monthly loses 35% annually; small improvements in retention compound dramatically (1% improvement = 3% more annual revenue).
- Expansion revenue (upsell, cross-sell, seats to existing customers) often exceeds new customer revenue in mature companies; Salesforce's model is 50%+ expansion revenue.
- Referral programs (customers referring other customers) are high-quality, low-cost acquisition; referred customers have higher retention and LTV than acquired customers.
- Customer advocacy (customers becoming public promoters: speaking at conferences, case studies, testimonials) drives inbound leads and word-of-mouth.

## Apex practices
- Build a customer health score (product usage, support tickets, revenue trajectory, contract renewal timing) and proactively engage low-health customers; intervention is 10x cheaper than churn.
- Create an upsell/cross-sell playbook (which products sell together, which customers are ready to expand, what messaging resonates) and review deal-by-deal.
- Implement a referral program (incentivize customers to refer others: free time, discount, commission) and make it frictionless (one-click referral, automatic reward tracking).
- Build a customer advisory board (10-15 power users who meet quarterly) and leverage for testimonials, case studies, and product feedback.

## Pitfalls
- Treating customer success as support; customer success is proactive engagement (check-ins, training, value realization) not reactive (ticket resolution).
- Chasing new customers while existing customers churn; a 90% retention improvement is cheaper than a 90% CAC improvement.
- Assuming advocacy and referrals are natural; most customers won't advocate without clear incentives and easy mechanisms.
