# Stress Management

## Scope
Regulating acute and chronic stress: physiology, recovery, cognitive reframing, and burnout prevention.

## Core principles
- Stress plus recovery equals growth; stress without recovery equals damage — the Yerkes-Dodson curve means moderate arousal helps performance, but chronic activation without offset drives the cortisol pathology Sapolsky documents (immune suppression, memory impairment, cardiovascular load).
- Perceived control and predictability modulate stress more than the stressor itself (Sapolsky's baboon and Whitehall studies): the same workload with autonomy is stimulating, without it is corrosive — target the controllability, not just the load.
- Stress appraisal is causally powerful: viewing arousal as the body mobilizing resources ("challenge" vs. "threat" appraisal, Crum's mindset studies) measurably changes cardiovascular response and performance — the belief is part of the mechanism.
- The fastest lever on the autonomic nervous system is respiration: extended exhales activate the parasympathetic brake within minutes (physiological sigh — double inhale, long exhale — Huberman/Balban 2023 found ~5 min/day outperformed meditation for mood).
- Burnout is not tiredness; it's the triad of exhaustion, cynicism/depersonalization, and reduced efficacy (Maslach), and it's driven by six workplace mismatches — workload, control, reward, community, fairness, values — not by insufficient self-care.

## Apex practices
- Schedule recovery like training: daily micro-recovery (walks, breath work), weekly full disengagement (psychological detachment from work predicts recovery better than time off per se — Sonnentag's research), and periodic longer resets.
- Do a "worry audit": write the stressor, the controllable component, and the next physical action — converting diffuse rumination into either action or explicit acceptance (the core move of Stoic dichotomy of control and modern CBT alike).
- Protect sleep first: sleep loss amplifies amygdala reactivity ~60% (Walker's lab); no stress technique compensates for chronic sub-7-hour sleep.
- Use exercise as the broad-spectrum intervention: 20–30 minutes of moderate cardio acutely lowers anxiety and chronically raises the stress threshold — effect sizes rival first-line treatments for mild-moderate anxiety/depression.

## Pitfalls
- Treating burnout with a vacation: if the six mismatches are untouched, the burnout resumes in weeks — the job design is the pathogen.
- Using alcohol or doomscrolling as "decompression" — both fragment sleep and raise next-day baseline anxiety, compounding the cycle.
- Stoicism misread as suppression: bottling emotion raises physiological load (Gross's research); reappraisal works, suppression backfires.
