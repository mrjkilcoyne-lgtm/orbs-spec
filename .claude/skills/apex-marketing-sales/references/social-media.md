# Social Media

## Scope
Building brand presence and customer engagement on social platforms: platform selection, content strategy, community management, and measuring impact.

## Core principles
- Different platforms serve different purposes: Twitter/X for thought leadership and live discussion, LinkedIn for B2B brand, TikTok for cultural relevance and younger audiences, Instagram for visual storytelling.
- Engagement on social is compressed (24-hour shelf life for a post); volume matters — 5 posts per week beats 1 post per week even if each individual post is lower quality.
- Virality is unpredictable but seeding matters; posts shared by internal advocates (employees, investors, customers) get algorithm boost; a post shared by 10 accounts reaches 10x more people.
- Social proof compounds; users follow accounts that have momentum (high engagement). Early follower count is slow, but engagement attracts more followers.
- Authenticity beats polish; audiences prefer behind-the-scenes, personal content over corporate-speak. Founders sharing their journey beats a marketing team's polished announcements.

## Apex practices
- Choose 1-2 primary platforms (Twitter and LinkedIn for B2B, TikTok and Instagram for B2C) and invest depth there instead of shallow presence everywhere.
- Post consistently on a cadence (daily for Twitter, 3x/week for LinkedIn) with a mix of content (educational, personal, promotional — 80/20 rule: 80% non-promotional value, 20% ask).
- Build an internal advocacy program (make it easy for employees to share content); employee reach is often 5-10x larger than company reach.
- Measure reach (impressions), engagement (likes, retweets, comments, shares), and business impact (clicks to website, signup attribution); vanity metrics (followers) are secondary.

## Pitfalls
- Spamming promotional content; audiences tune out accounts that are all ads. Value first, promotion second.
- Ignoring platform norms; LinkedIn-style formal content fails on TikTok; TikTok-style casual content fails on LinkedIn.
- Over-managing social presence with approval hierarchies; social is fast, and slow approval processes kill relevance.

## Tools & references
Buffer, Hootsuite, Later (scheduling), Sprout Social (enterprise), Twitter Advanced Search (finding conversations), Zapier (automation), LinkedIn's creator tools, TikTok for Business.
